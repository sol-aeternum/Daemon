# dev2 vertical-slice pilot, 2026-11-05

**Status:** ingest complete for 14 of 100 dev2 questions. Labels are **provisional until the owner spot-check**.
No retrieval, exposure, answer or judge step ran on any dev2 question. This report authorises nothing further.
Pre-registration: `tests/benchmark_longmemeval/dev2_pilot_preregistration.md`, including its 2026-11-05 amendment.
Evidence: `dev2_pilot_2026-11-05_evidence.json` (IDs, counts, timings, costs and hashes; no text).

## Result

- **Full dev2 ingest is practical:** about **7.2 hours and $29** on this laptop at concurrency 8.
- **One real defect found:** extraction replies cut off at the 2,000-token cap lost whole chunks (#670). Fixed for this pilot by #671, unmerged.
- **Resume works:** interrupted and resumed with no lost or duplicated sessions.
- **Open question:** the labeller marked 4 of 14 questions not answerable from the store. Those are either real retention gaps or an over-strict labeller; the spot-check decides.

## The c=8 stop and #670

The first c=8 run stopped at 161 of 392 sessions. One session, `sharegpt_xxk9JDz_21`, failed on all 4 attempts: 1.02% against 392, counted cumulatively across invocations under a rule fixed before the first resume. That met the 1% hard stop. It cost $0.96 and took 18.5 minutes.

Each failed attempt was a pair of calls stopping at exactly 2,000 output tokens. The code parsed these replies without checking `finish_reason`, so the chunk failed with no recorded reason. The same session failed 4 times in Phase D.

The owner chose to investigate, then to raise the cap to 4,000 and detect truncation. On restart at the new pin:

- all 392 sessions completed;
- no truncation diagnostics fired;
- **one reply reached 2,240 output tokens**, above the old cap, which supports the diagnosis.

`finish_reason: length` has still never been directly observed, because nothing truncated after the fix. The detection path is tested only with fakes.

**Comparability:** c=1 and c=4 ran at the 2,000 cap (`626819d1`) and c=8 at 4,000 (`bbf614c0`). Throughput and cost are comparable, since only 9 of 5,382 calls reached the old cap before the restart. Memories can differ from what the old cap would have produced only on chunks that would otherwise have failed.

## Projection for full dev2

The harness ingests per question, so a session shared by two questions runs twice: **4,776 sessions**, not the 4,574 unique sessions in `fixtures/dev2_freeze.md`.

| Item | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Wall time | **≈ 7.2 h** at c=8 | 662 sessions/hour |
| Ingest spend | **≈ $29** | $0.0061 per session. Lower than the note's $38–41 |
| Remaining 86 questions only | ≈ 6.2 h, ≈ $25 | Only if pilot data is reused (see below) |
| Labelling, 100 questions | ≈ $0.04, ≈ 5 min sequential | Pilot rates |
| Human spot-check | 15 questions, ≈ 75 min | 5 min each is an assumption |
| Database | ≈ 400 MB | 55 MB scaled by question count |

These extrapolate 14 questions at one time of day. Provider conditions may differ. Any further truncating chunk stops the run until resumed, and each such stop needs attention.

**Pilot reuse is not available as things stand.** c=1 and c=4 ran at a different code pin, and checkpoints pin dataset bytes, so the harness can't continue a full run from them. Reuse would need separately reviewed work. Plan on a full run; the $4–5 saving probably isn't worth that work.

## Platform recommendation

**Laptop, with resume.** About 7 hours fits one sitting, the machine had ample headroom at c=8, and resume is proven. A homelab or VPS offers little, because the bound is provider latency and not local capacity. **Never production Hetzner.**
