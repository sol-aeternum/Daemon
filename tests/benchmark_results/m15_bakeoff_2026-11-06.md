## What went wrong

### B and C were refused locally
All 100 calls failed in about 10 ms and none reached a provider. LiteLLM raises `UnsupportedParamsError` for `reasoning_effort` on these models: its model table doesn't list reasoning for them, while `config/model_routing.json` does. Tests used fakes and the pre-run review read the path without running it, so neither caught this. #681 tracks it. A later contract test found the same refusal on 4 of the 10 `council` models.

### The ledger overstates spend
| | Calls | Ledger | Provider-reported |
|---|---|---|---|
| B and C, refused locally, nothing sent | 100 | USD 0.0608 | USD 0 |
| A, successful | 50 | USD 0.0031 | USD 0.0017 |
| **Total** | 150 | **USD 0.0639** | **USD 0.0017** |

Cap: USD 0.50. Two separate causes:
1. **A local refusal settles at the full hold.** The dispatch failure handler charges the reserved amount for any exception, because it can't tell "never sent" from "sent, outcome unknown" (`compute_runtime.py:2691-2699`).
2. **A successful call settles at the route's price ceiling,** not at the provider's reported cost. Arm A's 10,121 input and 1,039 output tokens at the ceiling, rounded up per call, give exactly 3,109 micro-USD against 1,685 reported.

Both paths are shared with production. See the investigation on #681. No fix has been made.

### The failure stop didn't fire
The limit of 10 consecutive failures counted across arms, and arm order rotates, so Luna's successes reset it. The run sent every refused request instead of stopping after 10. It's now counted per arm, by owner ruling, with a regression test.
